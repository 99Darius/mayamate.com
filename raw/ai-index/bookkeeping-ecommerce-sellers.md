# Who AI recommends for bookkeeping service for ecommerce sellers

I asked 7 buying questions about bookkeeping service for ecommerce sellers across 4 AI-assistant configurations and read all 31 answers (28 from the main run plus 3 from an earlier partial run of the same questions). Measured 2026-09-23. **Recommended share** is the share of those answers that named a company at least once — not a ranking, not a review, just a count of who the assistants actually put in front of a buyer.

_Hand-checked against the verbatim answers before publishing. The table lists bookkeeping firms only. Accounting software is named more often than any of them — QuickBooks in 22 of 31 answers, Xero in 21, A2X in 16, Link My Books in 7 — but a buyer asking for a bookkeeping service is not asking which ledger to buy, so the software sits here, not in the table._

**One thing worth noticing.** Ask for a bookkeeper and the assistants answer with a software stack first: "use QuickBooks Online or Xero, connect A2X, then hire someone." A2X's site is tied with CoCountant's as the most-linked domain in the whole category (11 of 31 answers each) — an integration tool, level with the best-cited firm. Among firms, the four assistants disagree sharply: Bookkeeper360 is in all 7 Gemini answers and 1 of 8 from ChatGPT (gpt-4o-mini); EcomBalance is in 6 of 8 Perplexity answers and none of the 8 from ChatGPT (gpt-5); CoCountant is in 7 of 8 on Perplexity, 0 of 7 on Gemini, and every answer that names it also links to it — Perplexity is reading its site directly. Xendoo is the only firm that every one of the four assistants names in at least two of its answers.
