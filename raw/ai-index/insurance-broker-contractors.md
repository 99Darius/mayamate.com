**One thing worth noticing.** The buyer asked for a *broker*. The assistants answered with carriers (The Hartford, Hiscox, Liberty Mutual, Progressive, Nationwide), digital-first insurers (Next, Thimble) and online marketplaces (Simply Business, Insureon). Only five actual brokerages make the table — four global ones (Marsh, Gallagher, HUB, Lockton) and one contractor specialist — and no regional independent broker is named in any of the 28 answers. The assistants also disagree with each other more here than in any category I have measured so far: Insureon is in 6 of 7 ChatGPT (gpt-5) answers and 1 of 7 on Gemini; The Hartford is in 6 of 7 on Gemini and Perplexity and 1 of 7 on ChatGPT (gpt-5); Simply Business is in all 7 Perplexity answers, 1 of 7 on Gemini and 0 of 7 on ChatGPT (gpt-4o-mini). A contractor gets a different shortlist depending on which assistant they open.
